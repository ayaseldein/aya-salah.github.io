01 / About

Rigorous reporting.
Clear perspective.

My work sits at the intersection of financial journalism, banking research and macroeconomic reporting. I translate complex financial material into useful, accurate journalism for professional audiences and wider readers.

At FirstBank, I analyse financial statements and performance indicators, develop data-led banking coverage and contribute to its English-language platform. Previously at Amwal Al Ghad English, I covered economics, investment, trade, energy and markets.

I bring an Egyptian reporting background to stories with wider GCC implications — from the competitive position of Gulf banks to cross-border investment and regional business developments.
