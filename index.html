01 / Profile

The story
behind the
story.

Good financial journalism starts with the right question, not just the latest figure.

I’m a Cairo-based financial journalist and senior English economic analyst specialising in banking performance, monetary policy, foreign exchange and business reporting. My work combines financial statement analysis, data verification and accessible, precise storytelling.

At FirstBank, I produce data-led banking analysis and contribute to the development of its English-language platform. Previously at Amwal Al Ghad English, I covered Egyptian and international economic developments, including financial markets, investment, energy and policy.
