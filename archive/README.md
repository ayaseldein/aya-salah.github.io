# Article archive — Aya Elsayed

This folder preserves a local, version-controlled record of selected published work.

## Stored files

- **articles.json**: Original article titles, publisher, credits, publication dates or periods, topics, URLs, and independently written article summaries.
- **article-index.csv**: Portable article catalogue that can be opened in Excel or other spreadsheet applications.

Each entry has two website routes:
1. **Local summary**: `story.html?id=<slug>` — loads content from `articles.json` within this repository, so the summary still exists if a publisher link fails.
2. **Original publication**: a direct outbound URL to the publisher's article.

## Important limitation: These are NOT complete article backups

The article summaries and metadata are local copies. The original article body, images, layout, and publisher content **have not** been mirrored. If the external article disappears, this portfolio retains the summary and publication citation, **not its full text**.

For complete future-proof backups, provide your own original drafts, exported PDFs, manuscript files, or republication-authorised text. We can store those in a dedicated `archive/originals/` directory and link them from the local article page, while preserving the publisher URLs.

## Credit policy

Amwal Al Ghad credits reflect the bylines found on publisher pages where verified. FirstBank entries are identified as **publication coverage**; an individual author credit is not asserted without evidence.
